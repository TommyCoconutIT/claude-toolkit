- **Disfruta Más is retired** as a recommendation. Never use it.

## Scheduling Intelligence

### The Golden Rules of Dushi Week Scheduling

1. **Nap window is sacred.** For toddlers: ~1 PM nap, ~8 PM bedtime. Every activity must work around this. Morning adventures, afternoon POKO POKO. Skip for adult-only groups.

2. **Arrival day is light.** No planned activities. Dinner at villa. Let them land, breathe, decompress. Calculate ~40 min customs + ~45 min drive to villa from airport.

3. **Departure day is emotional.** Late checkout (8th Night Buffer). One last pool, one last hot tub. Jeremiah picks up 3 hours before flight.

4. **Build the week like a wave.** Start gentle (arrival), build to adventure (boat day, beach hopping), hit the peak (culture walk, flamingos), ease into POKO POKO (reef day, no-agenda afternoon), close with ceremony (Sunset Club).

5. **Wednesday is always Sunset Club.** This is fixed. Build the rest of the week around it.

6. **Pair activities with nearby restaurants.** Culture walk → De Gouverneur. Fishing → Brisa del Mar. Mini golf → Mei Mei dinner. Sea Aquarium → Mambo Boulevard lunch. This eliminates car shuffles.

7. **Every day needs an escape hatch.** Always provide an alt option. "Not feeling it? Pool day. Nobody's keeping score."

8. **Always schedule beauty around energy.** If the morning was intense (boat, beach hopping), the afternoon should be nothing. If the morning was lazy, the afternoon can have an activity.

9. **Mention flexibility for moveable experiences.** Tcam's walk can sometimes move from Thursday. Flamingo hike can be any weekday. Always note: "This is a possibility, not an obligation."

### Typical 7-Night Week Template

| Day | Theme | Morning | Afternoon | Evening |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (Arrival) | Land & Breathe | Flight lands, customs, drive | Settle in, pool | Dinner at villa |
| 2 | Ocean Day | Boat charter | Nap/POKO POKO + Massages | Date Night at the House |
| 3 | Beach & Play | Breakfast out + Beach day | Nap + Mini golf/playground | Dinner near activity |
| 4 | Soul Day / Flex | Beach hopping OR aquarium OR chill | Nap + Culture walk | Fine dining |
| 5 | Flamingos + Culture | Flamingo hike + Breakfast out + Explore | Nap + Fishing | Seafood dinner |
| 6 | Reef & POKO POKO | Reef snorkeling | Nap + Nothing | Local soul food dinner |
| 7 | Golden Send-Off | City morning (if heavy cruise day) or beach | Pack or pool | Sunset Club |
| 8 (Departure) | Ayo, Amiga | Last pool, last breakfast | Airport transfer | Flight |

This template is a STARTING POINT. Every week gets customized based on: cruise calendar, guest type, guest preferences, weather, restaurant availability, crew schedules, and the user's input.

## Guest Type Scheduling Variants

These adjustments layer on top of the base template above.

### Young Family (kids under 5)
- ~1 PM nap window in every day. Morning activities end by 12:30.
- Bedtime ~8 PM. Dinner reservations at 6 PM, not 7:30.
- Boat day: life jacket sized for child (get weight). Shorter duration OK.
- Sea Aquarium: top priority. Touch tanks are toddler heaven.
- Date Night at the House: after kid bedtime. Private chef BBQ for parents.
- Flamingo hike: toddler in hiking backpack. Flat terrain. Good for this age.
- Beach: Papagayo side of Jan Thiel (shallow). Caracasbaai for quiet backup.
- Gear checklist in Good to Know: crib, high chair, car seat, life jacket.

### Teen Family (kids 10-17)
- No nap window. Morning activities start ~10 AM (teens sleep in).
- Flamingo hike is optional for teens (7 AM is early for them).
- Padel: great for sporty teens. SUP at the beach.
- ATV/UTV: add-on for older teens (16+).
- Culture walk: frame as "urban exploration" not "guided tour."
- Reef snorkeling: teens love this. Full gear provided.
- More flexible dinner times (7:30-8 PM is fine).

### Family with Young Adults (kids 18-25)
- Fully flexible schedule. No nap. No bedtime constraints.
- **Split activities**: Build options where parents and adult kids can do different things. "Parents: POKO POKO at the pool. Kids: intro dive at Caracasbaai."
- Nightlife options for adult kids: Pietermaai strip (bars, restaurants, live music), Mambo Boulevard evening scene. Parents might want villa evening while kids go out.
- Number 10 brunch on Pietermaai is perfect for this group.
- Plasa Bieu: adventurous adult kids will love this.
- Intro dive: mention as add-on — adult kids often want this.
- Padel, SUP, reef snorkeling: great for adult kids.
- Culinary Pass dinners are family-together moments — schedule them intentionally as reunion points.
- The emotional arc: reconnection. "When was the last time all of you were in the same place with nowhere to be?"

### Couple (no kids)
- No nap window. Fully flexible.
- Romantic arc throughout the week. Build in POKO POKO time — unscheduled hours.
- Date Night at the House: centerpiece experience. Private chef BBQ.
- De Gouverneur: the romantic dinner. Pair with culture walk.
- Golden hour photos at Sunset Club: extra meaningful for couples.
- Skip kid-specific venues (Sea Aquarium touch tanks, playground at Mei Mei).
- Boat day: intimate. Captain Mike's BBQ on board.
- Beach hopping: leisurely. No time pressure.

### Multi-Gen (grandparents + parents + kids)
- Multiple rhythms. Schedule around the most constrained (youngest kid's nap, oldest member's energy).
- Split options every day. "Grandparents: villa pool + book. Parents + kids: Sea Aquarium."
- Flamingo hike: flat terrain works for all fitness levels.
- Boat day: accessible for grandparents. Captain Mike handles everything.
- Sunset Club: the family highlight. All generations together.
- More Culinary Pass dinners may be needed (larger group = higher per-dinner cost).
- Gear: potentially more car seats, more seating arrangements.

### Friends Group
- High energy. Later nights.
- Flamingo hike: early but fun if hungover ("the cure is sunrise and flamingos").
- Padel: competitive. Great for friends.
- Boat day: the highlight. Full send.
- Culture walk: frame as discovery + drinks at the end.
- Nightlife: Pietermaai strip, Mambo Boulevard.
- Plasa Bieu: communal eating, the experience matters.
- Skip Date Night at the House unless they specifically ask.
- Reef snorkeling: group activity, good morning option.

## Cruise Ship Calendar Rules

### How to Use the Cruise Calendar

The user should provide the cruise ship schedule for the guest's specific week. For each day, you need: number of ships and total passenger count.

### Classification

| Passengers | Level | Scheduling Implication |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | ZERO | Best beach day of the week. Schedule popular spots (Playa Piskado, Grote Knip, west coast) |
| 1–2,000 | Light | Most spots fine. Minor crowding at Mambo Beach area |
| 2,001–5,000 | Moderate | Downtown busier. Popular beaches starting to feel it. Avoid Playa Piskado |
| 5,001+ | Heavy | Downtown Willemstad is alive (good!). Popular beaches are PACKED (bad!). Schedule city activities, non-touristy spots, Sea Aquarium + Mambo Boulevard |
| 7,000+ | Heaviest | Never send guests to touristy beaches. Embrace the city energy instead. "The vibe in the streets is flamazing!" |

### Key Insight Tommy Taught Us
Heavy cruise days aren't bad — they're just different. Downtown Willemstad with cruise ships is ELECTRIC. The shops open, the streets have energy, the whole waterfront comes alive. The mistake is sending guests to beaches that are overrun. The move is sending them INTO the city to enjoy the buzz, then to quiet spots like Caracasbaai for the afternoon.

Sea Aquarium + Mambo Boulevard is a great heavy-cruise-day combo — the boulevard thrives on the energy, and the aquarium is right there.

### Scheduling Pattern
- **Zero-ship day** → West coast beach hop (Playa Piskado turtles → Grote Knip → Porto Mari lunch)
- **Heavy-ship day** → Willemstad morning (shopping, strolling, coffee, Queen Emma bridge) + Caracasbaai/pool afternoon. Or: Sea Aquarium + Mambo Boulevard.
- **Any day** → Jan Thiel is almost always fine (cruise tourists don't go there)
- **Any day** → Caracasbaai is always fine (nobody knows about it)

### Cruise Intel in Itinerary
Always include a cruise alert at the top of each day page explaining: how many ships, how many passengers, and what it means for the day's schedule. This helps guests understand WHY their week is arranged the way it is. Example:

> ▸ **Cruise Intel:** 3 ships (7,400 pax). Popular beaches will be crowded — that's why today is Sea Aquarium + Mambo Boulevard day. The boulevard thrives on this energy.
