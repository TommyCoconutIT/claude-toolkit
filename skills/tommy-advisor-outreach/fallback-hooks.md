# Fallback Hooks — when no contact-specific signal is found

The research subagent caps at 3 searches. If it can't surface a signal that meets the four criteria (specific, recent, verifiable, relevant), it returns `no_signal=true`.

In that case, the email-writer uses an **agency-level hook** from this file instead of a contact-specific opener. The email still has to feel observed — but the observation is about the agency, not the individual.

Mark the Airtable record with `Signal Confidence = no signal` so Boy knows the email was written without a personal hook.

**V7.0 numbers used throughout:** 4.99★ / 700+ stays / 204 of 700 rebooked / 8 estates in Jan Thiel.

---

## Format

For each agency below: a one-line hook the email-writer can use as Line 1 of the body, in place of "the signal."

If an agency below isn't listed, the email-writer should write a generic-but-specific hook based on what the agency's website says they do — never use "I came across your work" or any banned opener.

## When the contact's role is known but agency isn't above

Use this template:

> *"[Role-specific observation about what they do all day]. We're the Curaçao Private Resort built to make that [easier / quieter / faster] — 8 estates, 4.99 stars, 700 stays."*

Examples:
- VP Supplier Relations: *"You spend your week vetting brands. We saved you the meeting — here's the receipt: 204 of 700 rebooked before they left."*
- Independent advisor on Fora/Gifted/etc.: *"Your platform's Curaçao bench is thin. We fill it. 8 estates, brand-led, 4.99 stars."*

---

## When invoking The Tommy Coconut Promise as a fallback hook

For high-trust / UHNW-leaning agencies, lead with The Promise. It's the most operationally confident statement TC makes — and the V7.0 advisor-channel default:

> *"Here's the operational guarantee your clients haven't been offered before. Day 4 of every Dushi Week, we check in. If even one of eight wishes didn't land, the full balance is back in their account the same day. The bank has never sent the wire. 700 stays. 4.99 stars."*

Close with: `Vacation is holy. We protect yours.`

---

## What every fallback hook must do

1. Reference the AGENCY's known position, not the person.
2. Skip the personal flattery.
3. Land the Curaçao gap or the Promise in line 1.
4. Drop one verified stat (4.99 stars / 700+ stays / 204 of 700 / 8 estates / Jan Thiel).
5. Stay under 25 words for line 1.
6. Never call the advisor's clients "family." Use "couples / friends groups / families with kids" or "your clients" or "your strangers."
