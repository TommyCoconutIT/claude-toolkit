About Disfruta Más

Disfruta Más. Green food truck, Caracasbaaiweg 187. Women-run. Fresh batidos, your way — sugar or no sugar, milk or no milk. Venezuelan arepas. Best smoothies on the island. Go in the morning for the freshest fruit. Dushi.

10:30 AM — Playa Piskado

Wild sea turtles swim right up to shore as fishermen clean their catch. This is not an aquarium. This is the ocean doing what it does. Saturday brings local families to the beach — expect lively energy, music, kids in the water. The turtles are unbothered. Bring the snorkel gear, the water is clear.

Pro tip. Watch the road for the small roadside stands and trucks selling pastechi — deep-fried hand pies filled with cheese, beef, chicken, tuna, or ham. The Curaçao breakfast snack you carry in a paper bag and fight over who gets the last cheese one. Three or four dollars each, cash only at the stands.

12:00 PM — Grote Knip

THE postcard beach. Turquoise water so bright it looks edited. Dramatic cliffs framing a perfect cove. This is the family photo that ends up on the wall in [Hometown].

~6:30 PM — Dinner at De Gouverneur

The building dates to 1737 — an 18th-century colonial mansion in Otrobanda, right at Anna Bay with a full view of the Handelskade. Proper setting, proper service. Signature: Keshi Yena — the Curaçao national dish. The Cuban Banana Soup is legendary.

Pro tip. Book the window table — the Handelskade view across the harbour at night is the point of this restaurant. Order the Cuban Banana Soup first. The Swinging Old Lady Brewery next door names their beers after the Queen Emma bridge; their beer is on the menu. After dinner, Punda Vibes is three minutes across the bridge — the pontoon that swings open for the boats. This is the celebration-feel dinner of the week.

About De Gouverneur

Otrobanda, Anna Bay, view over the Handelskade pastels. Keshi Yena (the Curaçao national dish — chicken stewed inside a hollowed wheel of Edam), Cuban Banana Soup, fresh fish, ribeye, Caribbean classics. Open daily, dinner 5:30–10 PM.

Right next door is the Swinging Old Lady Brewery — Otrobanda's craft brewery, named after the Queen Emma pontoon bridge. Their beer is on the menu.
